Setup

Three steps, zero accounts.

Pair your devices once on your local network and let them reconnect automatically whenever you sit down at your Mac.

01 / INSTALL

Open both companion apps

Launch BridgeMac on macOS and install the Android companion on your phone or tablet.

02 / PAIR

Scan the local QR code

Use the Android app’s built-in QR scanner to exchange local cryptographic keys directly with your Mac.

03 / CONNECT

Work across both screens

Approve only the permissions you want—notifications, 2FA SMS, screen mirror, or file drop—and start working.
