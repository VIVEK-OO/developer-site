Privacy Policy

Applies to BridgeMac (Android & macOS) · Published by IndieMac Labs · Last Updated: November 2026

1. Core Principle: Local-Only & Peer-to-Peer

BridgeMac and its macOS companion application are built with a strict privacy-first, local-only architecture:

  • No Cloud Storage or Accounts: We do not require account registration, and we do not host, collect, store, or upload your files, SMS messages, clipboard items, screen frames, or notifications to any external cloud servers.
  • Direct Encrypted Connection: All communication between your Android device and your paired Mac happens directly over your local Wi-Fi network or USB cable using TLS and authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption.

2. Android Permissions & How Data Is Handled

Each permission is optional and requested only for the specific continuity feature you choose to enable:

A. SMS Permissions (RECEIVE_SMS, READ_SMS)

  • Purpose: Detects incoming Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) verification codes / OTPs and forwards them to your paired Mac so you can paste them quickly.
  • Handling: Incoming SMS messages are inspected locally in memory on your Android device. Extracted verification codes are sent exclusively to your paired Mac over the encrypted local channel and are never retained on external servers.

B. Notification Access (NotificationListenerService)

  • Purpose: Mirrors incoming notifications (calls, messages, and app alerts) from your Android device to your Mac desktop.
  • Handling: Notification payloads are processed in memory and forwarded directly to your paired Mac. No notification history is uploaded or sold.

C. Camera Permission (CAMERA)

  • Purpose: Used solely to scan the QR pairing code shown on your Mac to exchange cryptographic session keys.
  • Handling: Camera frames are analyzed strictly in real time on-device; no photos or video are saved or transmitted.

D. Screen Projection & Accessibility Input

  • Purpose: Enables live screen mirroring to your Mac and allows mouse/trackpad clicks and keyboard input from your Mac to control your paired Android device when mirroring is active.
  • Handling: Screen frames and input events travel only over the active encrypted peer-to-peer session and stop immediately when you end the session.

E. Local Network, Bluetooth & Foreground Service

  • Purpose: Discovers your paired Mac on the local network via mDNS/Bonjour or Bluetooth wake and maintains a reliable local connection while active.

3. Data Sharing, Analytics & Third Parties

  • We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal data with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • The application contains no third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral trackers, or cross-app analytics.

4. Data Security & Retention

  • Paired sessions are protected with authenticated encryption (AES-256-GCM) and local TLS device identity verification.
  • Pairing keys are stored locally using Android EncryptedSharedPreferences and the macOS Keychain.
  • Because we do not operate backend user databases, unpairing a device or uninstalling the app immediately removes all locally stored pairing keys and preferences.

5. Children’s Privacy

BridgeMac does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction), as the app collects no personal data on external servers.

6. Contact the Developer

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, data handling, or permissions, please contact IndieMac Labs at support@yourdomain.com .
