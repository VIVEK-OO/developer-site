Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pair my Android phone with my Mac?

Ensure both your Mac and Android device are connected to the same Wi-Fi network (or connected via USB cable). Open the companion app on your Mac, reveal the pairing QR code, and scan it using the Android app.

Why does the app ask for SMS or Notification permissions?

These permissions are strictly optional and used only to forward your incoming notifications and 2FA verification codes over the encrypted local connection to your paired Mac. Nothing is sent to cloud servers.

My Mac doesn’t appear automatically over Wi-Fi.

Some public or guest Wi-Fi networks enable AP/client isolation that blocks local mDNS/Bonjour discovery. Try switching to a private Wi-Fi network or connecting your phone to your Mac via USB.

How do I unpair or delete my stored keys?

Remove the paired device inside the Android or macOS app settings. Because all pairing credentials are stored locally in Android EncryptedSharedPreferences and the macOS Keychain, unpairing or uninstalling clears them permanently.

System Requirements

  • Android: Android 10+
  • macOS: macOS 13+ (Apple Silicon & Intel)
  • Network: Local Wi-Fi (same subnet for mDNS) or USB connection
